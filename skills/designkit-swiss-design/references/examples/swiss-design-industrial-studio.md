# WERK: Swiss design studio

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

An independent practice designing objects and identities that make sense in use.

Headline: Useful. Considered. Clear.

The primary action: Explore our work

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Numbered project labels and strict alignment communicate the practice's systematic approach.

### Material and geometry

Typographic order, disciplined alignment, asymmetry, and concise graphic contrast.

### Reading and interaction

Align text and imagery to a visible grid; communicate hierarchy through scale, weight, and position. Prefer immediate state changes and restrained fades; avoid decorative bounce.

## Typography

Heading: Space Grotesk
Body: Inter

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

## Adaptation

Align text and imagery to a visible grid; communicate hierarchy through scale, weight, and position.

Prefer immediate state changes and restrained fades; avoid decorative bounce.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Confusing precision with perfect symmetry.
- Adding decoration without a role.
- Using too many type sizes.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
