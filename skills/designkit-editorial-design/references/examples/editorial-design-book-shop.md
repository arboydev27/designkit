# folio books: Editorial design shop

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

A considered shelf of art, architecture, photography, and independent publishing.

Headline: Good books ask better questions.

The primary action: Explore the collection

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Product information becomes a tidy colophon beside generous, book-like visual framing.

### Material and geometry

Expressive type, thoughtful pacing, and the rhythm of a well-edited publication.

### Reading and interaction

Lead with a serif headline and a deliberate image crop; vary column widths around a clear reading sequence. Keep reading uninterrupted; use subtle fades only for an intentional content change.

## Typography

Heading: Cormorant Garamond
Body: DM Sans

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

- Ways of looking ($36): A small edition, made with care.
- Rooms and rituals ($48): A useful companion for everyday rituals.
- The independent issue ($24): A little character for your own space.

## Adaptation

Lead with a serif headline and a deliberate image crop; vary column widths around a clear reading sequence.

Keep reading uninterrupted; use subtle fades only for an intentional content change.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Letting composition disrupt reading order.
- Tiny captions on mobile.
- Using display type for long passages.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
