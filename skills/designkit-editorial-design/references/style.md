# Editorial design: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Editorial design organizes text and imagery into a deliberate reading experience. Expressive headlines, strong image direction, and a measured rhythm of dense and open space turn information into a narrative.

Expressive type, thoughtful pacing, and the rhythm of a well-edited publication.

- Expressive headlines
- Reading rhythm
- Art-directed imagery
- Rich hierarchy

## Three interpretations

### MARGIN (journal)

Independent stories about places, people, and the small details that change a point of view.

A large serif headline and varied column widths establish a publication's reading rhythm.

## Composition and behavior

Lead with a serif headline and a deliberate image crop; vary column widths around a clear reading sequence.

Keep reading uninterrupted; use subtle fades only for an intentional content change.

## Useful decisions

- Establish a comfortable reading measure.
- Treat captions and bylines as part of the system.
- Balance dramatic headings with quiet body text.

## Common mistakes

- Letting composition disrupt reading order.
- Tiny captions on mobile.
- Using display type for long passages.

## Suitability

- Digital magazines
- Culture publications
- Story-driven portfolios

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
