# SERIF atelier: Luxury typography studio

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

A small design practice shaping the voice and presence of thoughtful hospitality and cultural projects.

Headline: An identity, beautifully expressed.

The primary action: Explore our work

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Large typographic proportions turn the project introduction into the main visual.

### Material and geometry

Elegant display proportions, disciplined whitespace, and considered typographic detail.

### Reading and interaction

Treat large high-contrast type as the main image; maintain precise spacing and modest supporting controls. Use restrained, brief transitions; never delay navigation to imply prestige.

## Typography

Heading: Cormorant Garamond
Body: DM Sans

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

- A house by the water (Identity): Identity, concept, and visual direction.
- The gallery's new voice (Digital): Research shaped into a useful experience.
- A collection in print (Objects): A considered system, made to grow.

## Adaptation

Treat large high-contrast type as the main image; maintain precise spacing and modest supporting controls.

Use restrained, brief transitions; never delay navigation to imply prestige.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Tiny thin-stroked labels.
- Excessive tracking in body text.
- Assuming metallic color alone conveys quality.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
