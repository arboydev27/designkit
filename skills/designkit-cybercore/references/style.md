# Cybercore: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Cybercore is a loose contemporary aesthetic built around technological imagery, machine-like graphics, and digital intensity. Chrome forms, sharp geometry, and interface fragments can give an experience the feeling of a speculative computer system.

Terminal-like modularity, acid contrast, and purposeful technical annotations.

- Machine graphics
- Chrome accents
- Dense metadata
- Sharp geometry

## Three interpretations

### SYS.01 (archive)

A searchable archive of experiments in tools, networks, and human-machine collaboration.

Terminal labels and modular records give the archive a technical but readable structure.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cybercore-systems-archive/
Reference: examples/cybercore-systems-archive.md

### NODE (planner)

A local planning console for the tasks, notes, and decisions behind your next build.

Explicit task states make the technical appearance functional rather than decorative.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cybercore-build-planner/
Reference: examples/cybercore-build-planner.md

### KERNEL assembly (event)

A gathering for creative coders, tool builders, and people curious about the space between.

A modular timetable demonstrates technical density while preserving the reading order.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cybercore-creative-coding-meetup/
Reference: examples/cybercore-creative-coding-meetup.md

## Composition and behavior

Use modular terminal panels and technical typography; group dense information with consistent alignment.

Use immediate status updates and a brief focus highlight; do not flicker text or simulate failures.

## Useful decisions

- Use metadata to reinforce real information.
- Build a clear structure beneath the visual noise.
- Limit chrome to a few focal elements.

## Common mistakes

- Unreadable pseudo-technical labels.
- Constant flicker or flashing.
- Confusing decorative panels with interactive controls.

## Suitability

- Experimental music
- Digital art platforms
- Technology launches

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
