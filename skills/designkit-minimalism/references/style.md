# Minimalism: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Minimalist interfaces reduce visual elements to what supports the content and task. Careful typography, spacing, and proportion carry the identity when decoration is kept restrained.

Few decisions, expressed consistently, with generous space and clear purpose.

- Reduced elements
- Generous space
- Precise typography
- Restrained color

## Three interpretations

### still. (studio)

Spaces and objects designed around the things that make everyday life feel considered.

A restrained project index makes the work itself carry the visual identity.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/minimalism-architecture-studio/
Reference: examples/minimalism-architecture-studio.md

### OBJECT (shop)

Everyday objects with a clear purpose, honest materials, and no unnecessary detail.

Product labels and whitespace make selection easy without decorative containers.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/minimalism-everyday-shop/
Reference: examples/minimalism-everyday-shop.md

### LESS (journal)

Short essays about making fewer decisions and giving the remaining ones more care.

A narrow column and quiet hierarchy keep attention on the writing.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/minimalism-design-journal/

## Composition and behavior

Reduce to essential content and clear affordances; give spacing and typography explicit roles.

Prefer immediate navigation and subtle state feedback; avoid ornamental reveals.

## Useful decisions

- Make every spacing decision deliberate.
- Keep useful labels and interaction cues.
- Use one accent color with a clear role.

## Common mistakes

- Removing information users need.
- Equating empty space with good hierarchy.
- Making controls visually indistinguishable from text.

## Suitability

- Focused products
- Architecture studios
- Quiet commerce

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
