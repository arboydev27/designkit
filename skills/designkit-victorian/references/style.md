# Victorian: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Victorian-era design encompassed many movements and revivals rather than one uniform style. A contemporary Victorian-inspired interface often draws on ornate borders, decorative lettering, engraved imagery, and richly framed compositions.

Decorative framing and historic display character with contemporary reading clarity.

- Ornate borders
- Decorative lettering
- Engraved imagery
- Framed compositions

## Three interpretations

### THE CHRONICLE (journal)

Essays, curiosities, and correspondence for readers with an appetite for a closer look.

Decorative rules frame a contemporary reading column with clear article headings.

Reference: examples/victorian-literary-journal.md

### CABINET & CO. (shop)

Printed curiosities, considered keepsakes, and small editions for a well-loved shelf.

Ornamental product framing suggests a cabinet while shopping controls remain familiar.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/victorian-curiosity-shop/
Reference: examples/victorian-curiosity-shop.md

### THE SOCIETY (event)

A gathering of readers, makers, and people who believe a good question deserves company.

A framed invitation introduces the programme without turning detailed copy into blackletter.

## Composition and behavior

Frame a clear central hierarchy with ornamental rules; reserve blackletter for short display moments.

Keep ornament static and state changes direct; readable body type carries detailed information.

## Useful decisions

- Choose a specific historical reference to guide ornament.
- Reserve elaborate lettering for short headings.
- Simplify borders and framing on small screens.

## Common mistakes

- Treating all nineteenth-century styles as identical.
- Blackletter for navigation or long paragraphs.
- Ornament that competes with the content.

## Suitability

- Literary experiences
- Heritage-inspired brands
- Theatrical event pages

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
