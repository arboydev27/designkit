# NIGHTDRIVE: Synthwave event

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

An evening of electronic music, neon memories, and sounds from the future we once imagined.

Headline: The night has another horizon.

The primary action: Explore the programme

## Design reasoning

### Composition

A static horizon sets the mood behind a clearly structured event programme.

### Material and geometry

Retro-futurist horizons, luminous accent lines, and cinematic contrast.

### Reading and interaction

Set a horizon-like visual behind a clear foreground panel; use a limited pink-violet spectrum. Static horizons preserve the mood; keep interaction feedback brief and avoid flashing neon.

## Typography

Heading: Space Grotesk
Body: Space Mono

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Adaptation

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Neon text on bright gradients.
- Animating every light source.
- Overusing grid patterns behind content.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
