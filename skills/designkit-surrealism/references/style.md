# Surrealism: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Surrealism explores dreams, the unconscious, and unexpected connections between familiar things. In digital design, impossible scale and surprising juxtapositions can create a memorable visual world around a clear interface.

Unexpected scale and spatial relationships made believable by consistent lighting.

- Dream logic
- Unexpected scale
- Impossible spaces
- Strange juxtapositions

## Three interpretations

### OTHERWHERE (event)

An exhibition of impossible spaces, familiar objects, and things that refuse to behave.

An improbable arch becomes the visual anchor, while the exhibition details remain ordinary and clear.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/surrealism-exhibition/
Reference: examples/surrealism-exhibition.md

### soft matter (shop)

Small sculptural editions for the corner of your home that could use a question mark.

Unexpected product shapes carry the fantasy; cart controls use familiar affordances.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/surrealism-object-shop/
Reference: examples/surrealism-object-shop.md

### dream index (archive)

Browse a collection of spatial experiments, strange compositions, and speculative objects.

A structured searchable index offers a useful counterweight to the improbable imagery.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/surrealism-image-archive/
Reference: examples/surrealism-image-archive.md

## Composition and behavior

Use one improbable spatial composition, such as an arch around a floating form, beside a legible text block.

Reveal a changed object briefly; avoid endless floating or motion that competes with reading.

## Useful decisions

- Build around one strong visual contradiction.
- Keep navigation grounded and predictable.
- Let negative space heighten the unusual.

## Common mistakes

- Using arbitrary weirdness without an idea.
- Making essential content difficult to discover.
- Motion that cannot be paused or reduced.

## Suitability

- Art exhibitions
- Creative campaigns
- Experimental portfolios

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
