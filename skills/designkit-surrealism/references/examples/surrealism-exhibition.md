# OTHERWHERE: Surrealism event

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

An exhibition of impossible spaces, familiar objects, and things that refuse to behave.

Headline: Reality is only a suggestion.

The primary action: Explore the programme

## Design reasoning

### Composition

An improbable arch becomes the visual anchor, while the exhibition details remain ordinary and clear.

### Material and geometry

Unexpected scale and spatial relationships made believable by consistent lighting.

### Reading and interaction

Use one improbable spatial composition, such as an arch around a floating form, beside a legible text block. Reveal a changed object briefly; avoid endless floating or motion that competes with reading.

## Typography

Heading: Syne
Body: DM Sans

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

- The room without a floor (10:00): A conversation to begin the gathering.
- An object out of time (13:00): Time to make, listen, and try something.
- A conversation with a shadow (16:00): Bring the day's ideas together.

## Adaptation

Use one improbable spatial composition, such as an arch around a floating form, beside a legible text block.

Reveal a changed object briefly; avoid endless floating or motion that competes with reading.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Using arbitrary weirdness without an idea.
- Making essential content difficult to discover.
- Motion that cannot be paused or reduced.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
