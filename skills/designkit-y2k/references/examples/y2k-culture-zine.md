# orbit club: Y2K aesthetic journal

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

A small digital zine about internet culture, personal style, and collecting your own world.

Headline: Logging on to something good.

The primary action: Find a story

## Design reasoning

### Composition

A magazine layout balances glossy accents with enough calm space for reading.

### Material and geometry

Glossy pop shapes, early-digital optimism, and deliberate kitsch.

### Reading and interaction

Pair inflated or chrome-like shapes with compact product panels and playful display type. Short shine or press feedback is optional; keep chrome effects static under reduced motion.

## Typography

Heading: Space Grotesk
Body: Space Grotesk

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

- The charm of old websites (Essay): An observation worth spending time with.
- Everything transparent (Conversation): A conversation with a different point of view.
- A mixtape for the bus ride (Field notes): A small story with something to carry onward.

## Adaptation

Pair inflated or chrome-like shapes with compact product panels and playful display type.

Short shine or press feedback is optional; keep chrome effects static under reduced motion.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Relying on nostalgia for usability.
- Tiny decorative interface text.
- Gloss effects that reduce contrast.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
