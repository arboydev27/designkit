# Y2K aesthetic: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Y2K design revisits the optimistic digital imagery associated with the turn of the millennium. Glossy surfaces, silver accents, bubble shapes, and saturated color evoke an early internet vision of the future.

Glossy pop shapes, early-digital optimism, and deliberate kitsch.

- Glossy finishes
- Chrome details
- Bubble forms
- Digital nostalgia

## Three interpretations

### gloss.exe (shop)

Chrome charms, translucent objects, and a little early-internet optimism for your everyday.

Glossy shapes and compact shopping panels adapt the aesthetic to a real product hierarchy.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/y2k-accessory-shop/
Reference: examples/y2k-accessory-shop.md

### AFTERGLOW (event)

A pop night for shiny outfits, favorite songs, and friends you haven't met yet.

A poster-like heading sits above a practical, clearly labelled evening schedule.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/y2k-pop-night/
Reference: examples/y2k-pop-night.md

### orbit club (journal)

A small digital zine about internet culture, personal style, and collecting your own world.

A magazine layout balances glossy accents with enough calm space for reading.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/y2k-culture-zine/
Reference: examples/y2k-culture-zine.md

## Composition and behavior

Pair inflated or chrome-like shapes with compact product panels and playful display type.

Short shine or press feedback is optional; keep chrome effects static under reduced motion.

## Useful decisions

- Choose a small set of nostalgic motifs.
- Use silver and gloss as accent materials.
- Keep core controls consistent beneath the styling.

## Common mistakes

- Relying on nostalgia for usability.
- Tiny decorative interface text.
- Gloss effects that reduce contrast.

## Suitability

- Fashion drops
- Pop culture projects
- Music and nightlife

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
