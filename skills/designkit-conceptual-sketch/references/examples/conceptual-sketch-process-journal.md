# the draft room: Conceptual sketch journal

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

Conversations and unfinished notes from people who like figuring things out by making them.

Headline: Thinking, still in progress.

The primary action: Find a story

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Annotations belong beside a readable article column rather than on top of its text.

### Material and geometry

Visible thinking, hand-drawn framing, and an approachable workshop atmosphere.

### Reading and interaction

Use imperfect drawn boundaries and annotations around a clear conceptual structure. Make editing immediate; let line-like accents remain still rather than repeatedly drawing themselves.

## Typography

Heading: Space Grotesk
Body: Space Grotesk

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Content

- The value of a bad first sketch (Essay): An observation worth spending time with.
- A question worth keeping (Conversation): A conversation with a different point of view.
- Learning in the margins (Field notes): A small story with something to carry onward.

## Adaptation

Use imperfect drawn boundaries and annotations around a clear conceptual structure.

Make editing immediate; let line-like accents remain still rather than repeatedly drawing themselves.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Making finished features look broken.
- Decorative scribbles that obscure content.
- Using roughness to excuse inconsistent layout.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
