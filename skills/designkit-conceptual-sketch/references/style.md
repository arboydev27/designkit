# Conceptual sketch: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Conceptual sketch styling exposes the marks of thinking through hand-drawn lines, annotations, and rough diagrams. It can make an experience feel exploratory and human while finished navigation provides a stable frame.

Visible thinking, hand-drawn framing, and an approachable workshop atmosphere.

- Hand-drawn lines
- Visible annotations
- Rough diagrams
- Paper surfaces

## Three interpretations

### rough ideas (studio)

A design workshop turning questions, quick sketches, and conversations into useful possibilities.

Hand-drawn project framing exposes the thinking process without making navigation uncertain.

### FIELDNOTES (planner)

A working notebook for collecting questions, trying small experiments, and recording what you learned.

Editable tasks and drawn borders turn the workshop atmosphere into a useful planning interface.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/conceptual-sketch-project-notebook/
Reference: examples/conceptual-sketch-project-notebook.md

### the draft room (journal)

Conversations and unfinished notes from people who like figuring things out by making them.

Annotations belong beside a readable article column rather than on top of its text.

## Composition and behavior

Use imperfect drawn boundaries and annotations around a clear conceptual structure.

Make editing immediate; let line-like accents remain still rather than repeatedly drawing themselves.

## Useful decisions

- Give annotations a meaningful explanatory role.
- Use consistent line weights despite the rough finish.
- Keep interaction targets clear and generously sized.

## Common mistakes

- Making finished features look broken.
- Decorative scribbles that obscure content.
- Using roughness to excuse inconsistent layout.
