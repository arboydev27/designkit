# ma atelier: Wabi-sabi studio

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

A small architecture practice working with light, existing materials, and the character of place.

Headline: Space to simply be.

The primary action: Explore our work

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Off-center project compositions suggest an organic balance without disturbing navigation.

### Material and geometry

Tactile earth tones, modest serif type, and imperfect geometry with clear controls.

### Reading and interaction

Leave generous negative space and offset one visual anchor within a dependable reading order. Use brief fades for state changes; let stillness carry the atmosphere.

## Typography

Heading: Cormorant Garamond
Body: DM Sans

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Adaptation

Leave generous negative space and offset one visual anchor within a dependable reading order.

Use brief fades for state changes; let stillness carry the atmosphere.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Treating Japanese culture as a decorative shorthand.
- Low-contrast text on textured backgrounds.
- Adding artificial distress to every element.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
