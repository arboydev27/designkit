# Wabi-sabi: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Rooted in Japanese aesthetics, wabi-sabi values impermanence, modesty, and the character of things shaped by time. A digital interpretation can use natural textures, quiet spacing, and gentle asymmetry to suggest that material sensitivity.

Tactile earth tones, modest serif type, and imperfect geometry with clear controls.

- Natural textures
- Quiet asymmetry
- Earthy neutrals
- Unhurried space

## Three interpretations

### tsuchi (shop)

Small-batch vessels, formed by hand and made for the rituals of everyday life.

The product is a quiet material object, so a modest catalog and generous space support it.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/wabi-sabi-earthware/
Reference: examples/wabi-sabi-earthware.md

### After rain (journal)

A journal of walking, making, and noticing the things that time leaves behind.

A narrow reading column and restrained folio make room for reflective writing.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/wabi-sabi-rain-journal/
Reference: examples/wabi-sabi-rain-journal.md

### ma atelier (studio)

A small architecture practice working with light, existing materials, and the character of place.

Off-center project compositions suggest an organic balance without disturbing navigation.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/wabi-sabi-architecture/

## Composition and behavior

Leave generous negative space and offset one visual anchor within a dependable reading order.

Use brief fades for state changes; let stillness carry the atmosphere.

## Useful decisions

- Let a few tactile details carry the atmosphere.
- Use asymmetry within a dependable reading order.
- Give imagery room to breathe.

## Common mistakes

- Treating Japanese culture as a decorative shorthand.
- Low-contrast text on textured backgrounds.
- Adding artificial distress to every element.

## Suitability

- Ceramics and craft
- Thoughtful hospitality
- Slow living journals

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
