# Scrapbook: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Scrapbook design borrows the visual language of collected photographs, paper scraps, handwritten notes, and tape. Overlaps and small irregularities make the composition feel personal while an underlying structure keeps it navigable.

Handwritten accents, paper layering, and collected details rather than random clutter.

- Paper layers
- Handwritten notes
- Taped photographs
- Casual composition

## Three interpretations

### little things (journal)

A collection of happy accidents, small adventures, and things found along the way.

Paper layers and handwritten notes support a personal voice around readable articles.

### paper trail (studio)

Illustration, playful identities, and stories pieced together with care.

A collected portfolio gives every project a different paper frame within one navigation system.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/scrapbook-illustration-studio/

### keepsake club (shop)

Stationery for making lists, sending love, and saving a piece of an ordinary day.

Tape-like framing is used on products while purchase actions stay straight and legible.

## Composition and behavior

Arrange a few layered paper pieces with slight offsets; keep navigation and text baselines dependable.

Tiny press feedback makes paper feel tangible; keep larger collages still.

## Useful decisions

- Repeat a small set of paper and tape treatments.
- Keep important controls level and readable.
- Use handwriting for short expressive moments.

## Common mistakes

- Random rotation on every item.
- Overlapping essential text.
- Using handwriting for long paragraphs.

## Suitability

- Travel journals
- Personal portfolios
- Community storytelling

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
