# Claymorphism: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Claymorphism gives interface elements the rounded volume and matte finish of modeled clay. Soft inner highlights, broad shadows, and friendly color make forms feel inflated and approachable.

Puffy rounded surfaces and soft shadows with crisp, readable labels.

- Rounded volume
- Matte surfaces
- Soft shadows
- Friendly colors

## Three interpretations

### poppy (planner)

Make room for a few good habits without turning your life into a performance chart.

Inflated task cards make completion feel friendly while checkboxes keep the state explicit.

## Composition and behavior

Use rounded, inflated forms as a few friendly anchors, with open space around text and controls.

A short, critically damped press response suggests softness; reduce it to color feedback when requested.

## Useful decisions

- Keep lighting direction consistent.
- Pair dimensional illustrations with clear flat labels.
- Use spacious, comfortably sized controls.

## Common mistakes

- Making every component look like a toy.
- Soft shadows as the only control boundary.
- Oversized effects on dense tables.

- Learning products

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
