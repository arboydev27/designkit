# Cyberpunk: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Cyberpunk imagery places advanced technology within gritty, socially fractured futures. Digital interfaces inspired by it often combine dark urban surfaces, sharp neon accents, industrial labels, and signs of technological interference.

Dystopian contrast, angular surfaces, and an intentionally charged technical atmosphere.

- Neon accents
- Dark urban surfaces
- Industrial details
- Controlled interference

## Three interpretations

### NØ SIGNAL (event)

An independent electronic night at the edge of sound, code, and the city after dark.

Angular panels and charged contrast express the atmosphere around a readable timetable.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cyberpunk-night-festival/
Reference: examples/cyberpunk-night-festival.md

### NIGHT MARKET (shop)

Independent streetwear and objects for people moving through their own version of tomorrow.

A product grid channels technical intensity while prices and cart actions remain obvious.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cyberpunk-streetwear-shop/
Reference: examples/cyberpunk-streetwear-shop.md

### SECTOR 7 (planner)

A personal planning console for the experiments, projects, and ideas you want to bring into the world.

Task controls use explicit completion states rather than simulated warnings or glitches.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/cyberpunk-creative-console/
Reference: examples/cyberpunk-creative-console.md

## Composition and behavior

Use clipped corners, strong warning-like contrast, and technical alignment; retain an obvious reading path.

Use immediate state feedback; avoid glitch loops, flashing text, and fake security alerts.

## Useful decisions

- Use neon as a signal against quieter dark surfaces.
- Make technical labels convey real content.
- Keep glitch effects optional and brief.

## Common mistakes

- Flashing effects or persistent visual interference.
- Decorative warning states that look actionable.
- Dense neon text without resting space.

## Suitability

- Science-fiction worlds
- Gaming communities
- Experimental music events

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
