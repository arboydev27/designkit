# Ethereal: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Ethereal styling creates a sense of airiness through diffused light, delicate forms, and softly shifting color. The mood is dreamlike and spacious, with clear foreground elements keeping the experience usable.

Light, delicate forms and gentle color transitions with readable text.

- Diffused light
- Airy spacing
- Soft gradients
- Delicate forms

## Three interpretations

### lumière (studio)

A gentle practice of breath, movement, and making a little room for yourself.

Airy spacing and light forms give the offerings room without lowering text contrast.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/ethereal-wellness-practice/
Reference: examples/ethereal-wellness-practice.md

### DRIFT (planner)

An unhurried space to notice how the day feels and choose what would help.

Soft task panels use explicit states so the atmosphere never replaces usability.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/ethereal-reflection-planner/
Reference: examples/ethereal-reflection-planner.md

### halo objects (shop)

Objects for rest, reflection, and the corners of a day that need a softer touch.

Delicate framing surrounds tactile products while labels keep the shop straightforward.

## Composition and behavior

Use airy spacing and translucent light shapes, anchored by a high-contrast reading surface.

Short fades preserve softness; omit drifting backgrounds and parallax.

## Useful decisions

- Keep text on a stable, readable surface.
- Use soft blur around imagery rather than labels.
- Offer still versions of drifting effects.

## Common mistakes

- Very pale text on pale backgrounds.
- Slow animations that delay access to content.
- Making every edge indistinct.

## Suitability

- Fragrance and beauty
- Ambient music
- Poetic portfolios

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
