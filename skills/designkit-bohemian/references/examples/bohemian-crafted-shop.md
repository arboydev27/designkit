# sol & luna: Bohemian shop

DesignKit 2.0.0. Original fictional example, reusable under MIT; bundled fonts retain their original OFL licenses.

## Project context

Earth-made treasures and small-run objects with a story waiting to be carried onward.

Headline: Follow your wild.

The primary action: Explore the collection

## Design reasoning

### Composition

Organic arches and warm materials support the craft while a simple product grid stays useful.

### Material and geometry

Earth-made color, eclectic craft, and organic silhouettes used with restraint.

### Reading and interaction

Build a collected composition using warm materials, organic arches, and a few handmade details. Use brief press feedback; tactile patterns stay still behind text.

## Typography

Heading: Cormorant Garamond
Body: DM Sans

Treat this pairing as an interpretation. If the destination project already has a suitable typeface, preserve it unless the requested direction needs a different voice.

## Adaptation

Build a collected composition using warm materials, organic arches, and a few handmade details.

Use brief press feedback; tactile patterns stay still behind text.

Use the page structure for its context, not as a universal template. Keep the user's real content and required interactions.

## Review

- Combining cultural motifs without context.
- Using texture behind small text.
- Adding every natural material to one composition.

Check reading order and text contrast at phone width; ensure native controls retain keyboard and focus feedback. Preview tasks, carts, and schedules are local demonstrations, not backend integrations.
