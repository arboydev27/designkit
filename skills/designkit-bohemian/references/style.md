# Bohemian: DesignKit reference

Version 2.0.0. A contemporary interpretation of this direction. The three projects vary content, palette, and typography while preserving the style’s defining materials and composition. Treat suggested colors and typefaces as adaptable choices.

## Character

Contemporary bohemian styling combines warm colors, tactile materials, and an eclectic sense of collection. Handmade details and organic shapes can communicate informality without requiring a fixed visual formula.

Earth-made color, eclectic craft, and organic silhouettes used with restraint.

- Warm pigments
- Textile textures
- Eclectic layers
- Organic shapes

## Three interpretations

### sol & luna (shop)

Earth-made treasures and small-run objects with a story waiting to be carried onward.

Organic arches and warm materials support the craft while a simple product grid stays useful.

Reference: examples/bohemian-crafted-shop.md

### WANDER NOTES (journal)

A journal of people, places, and handmade things found when there is time to look.

Collected editorial shapes suggest travel without overwhelming longer stories.

Preview: https://designkit-vert.vercel.app/examples/bohemian-travel-journal/
Reference: examples/bohemian-travel-journal.md

### desert house (event)

A small creative gathering around drawing, shared meals, and learning from the landscape.

An organic poster composition introduces a practical, readable retreat schedule.

## Composition and behavior

Build a collected composition using warm materials, organic arches, and a few handmade details.

Use brief press feedback; tactile patterns stay still behind text.

## Useful decisions

- Connect patterns to a real material or story.
- Choose a limited set of warm pigments.
- Let organic imagery soften a clear grid.

## Common mistakes

- Combining cultural motifs without context.
- Using texture behind small text.
- Adding every natural material to one composition.

## Suitability

- Artisan shops
- Independent retreats
- Lifestyle journals

Choose a relevant interpretation or compose a new one for the actual project. Do not turn a visual style into a requirement to use a particular page structure.
